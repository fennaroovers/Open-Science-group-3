**Instructions for assembling the gearbox**

1) Collect the 3D printed parts

[image 1]

2) Collect the needed materials:
   - 2 long M3 screws
   - 4 M3 screws for the legs
   - 5 nuts
   - A circular adapter
   - Tape
   - 2 shafts
   - 2 shorts screws matching the diameter of the motor holes
   If any of these are unclear go to the next steps and first see what you will do with them and then look for the right pieces again.

3) Screw the motor into the lower plate

4) Put the two long screws in and fix them with tape.

5) Put the gearts and shafts in, look at the pictures for the order:

6) Put the circular adapter in the top part

7) Place the top part and put in the screw.
